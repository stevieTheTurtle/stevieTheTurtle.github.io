About

Media Engineer turned PhD researcher at Politecnico di Torino (CGVGroup), currently on a sandwich period at PUCRS in Brazil. My work sits on Embodied Conversational Agents: virtual humans that perceive, reason with LLMs and answer with believable body language.

Before code there was a violin. I still approach a render loop and a musical phrase the same way: structure first, then expression.
